Install Staff App

This shortcut opens the Staff workspace, not the Owner dashboard.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Name it PDK Staff, then tap Add.
  4. Open the new PDK Staff icon. If your staff session is still active, it opens your staff workspace; otherwise, sign in through the Staff Portal.
